    ⧗ Chronoscope

    You are watching a world whose history is completely deterministic. Every run of the same world plays out identically, year for year. Peoples settle, farm, multiply, fight, trade ideas, catch plagues, rise into empires and shatter into successor states, all from a handful of simple rules.

    Travel back in time and change one thing. Pick a tool on the left and click the map. A new timeline branches off, the future is rewritten, and you can compare it with the history you came from.

    • Chart: each coloured band is a people. Click or drag to travel in time. Gold marks are your interventions.
    • Chronicle: the future is already written (italics). After a change, new events are history you created, and erased ones you prevented.
    • Divergence map mode shows which lands are ruled by a different people than in the timeline you compare against.

    The rules

    • Land: soil feeds people; farming depletes it, fallow land recovers. Rivers and coasts are rich.
    • People: grow toward what the land can feed, starve in droughts, spill over into empty land.
    • Solidarity (Ibn Khaldun's asabiya): grows on contested frontiers, decays in comfortable interiors.
    • War: power = solidarity × population × technology, fading with distance from the capital. Strong neighbours take land.
    • Ideas: technology grows with population, raises harvests and reach, crosses borders, and is partly lost when realms fall.
    • Collapse: when an empire's solidarity stays low too long it shatters into successor states. Far provinces may rebel first.
    • Plague and climate: crowded cities breed epidemics; slow regional droughts and golden ages come and go.
